# AuPro campus survey plan

## Survey goal

Build an accurate, reviewable map of the College of Engineering area at Andhra University.

## Admin survey capture

An authorized admin can record:

- GPS path while walking.
- Building and entrance points.
- Official name and category.
- Short description.
- Opening hours when verified.
- Official contact details when verified.
- Accessibility notes.
- Photos only when reuse rights and approval are confirmed.

Raw GPS data is private to authorized admins. The system derives a cleaned path after removing noise, unnecessary detail, and identifying metadata. Only the derived verified path can be published.

## Student suggestions

Students can submit a suggested correction with a proposed place, text, optional photo, and map position. Suggestions are private until an admin reviews them. Admins can accept, edit, reject, or request more information.

## Draft workflow

1. Survey or suggestion is saved as a draft.
2. Admin checks name, position, category, contacts, photos, and accessibility details.
3. Admin accepts or edits the entry.
4. The approved place/path becomes public.
5. The source, reviewer, and last-reviewed date are retained.

## Quality checklist

- Building pin is at the correct building or entrance.
- Name matches official campus usage.
- Category is consistent.
- Contact details are institutional and verified.
- Hours have a source or are marked unknown.
- Photos have reuse approval.
- Accessibility notes are based on observation or official information.
- Raw GPS tracks are not exposed.
